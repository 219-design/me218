# ME218 PCB Ruler Mechanical Resources

Explore our demo designs in [Onshape](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/a66641dbc2e63d92f0bfc751/w/f00ac217e622a64d2a372b85/e/9fa5ab666376091f7bf63026). 

![Mechanical Demos](https://github.com/219-design/me218/blob/main/pcb-ruler/photos/mechanical.jpg)

This folder is for 3D printable resources and assembly instructions for add-ons to the PCB ruler.

Looking for a design related to an interdisciplinary project? Check out the [examples folder](https://github.com/219-design/me218/tree/lib/pcb-ruler/firmware/ruler_lib/examples).

---

The PCB ruler is also a great platform for **3D-printed attachments**.  

We’ve designed a **3D-printable clamp** that securely grips the ruler and can be adapted for a variety of uses — such as **tool holders**, **marking tools**, or other creative mechanical add-ons.
